# Code of Conduct

This project is a place for careful, practical work on how AI systems communicate. Participation is open to anyone who treats others with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, rules, and test results, not people.
- Be specific and charitable. Give reproducible examples when reporting a problem.
- Welcome people of any background, language, and experience level. Spanish and English are both normal here.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other people's personal information, and keep personal or confidential content out of issues, test cases, and examples.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests, and discussions. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and may restrict participation after repeated or serious violations.

To report a problem, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile, or open an issue that omits sensitive details and ask for a private channel. Reports are handled in confidence as far as practical.

This is a short original statement for a small project. Maintainers may later adopt a longer standard text.
