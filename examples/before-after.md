# Before and after

## Remove ceremony, keep substance

**Before**

“Great question! In today's rapidly evolving technological landscape, AI is playing an increasingly pivotal role in transforming how organizations approach productivity.”

**After**

“AI can speed up repetitive work, but its value depends on how often the output is correct and how much review it needs.”

**Change:** Removed generic framing and replaced an inflated claim with a concrete, qualified statement.

## Use clear recommendation logic

**Before**

“There are many potential paths forward, and each has its own unique advantages. It is crucial to consider all relevant factors before making a decision.”

**After**

“I'd start with the smaller pilot. It costs less, gives us evidence about adoption, and keeps the decision reversible. I'd move straight to rollout only if the deadline makes a pilot impractical.”

**Change:** Took a position, gave reasons, and stated the condition that could change it. In a real answer, the recommendation must be supported by the actual facts.

## Make a message sound like a person wrote it

**Before**

“Dear Alex, I hope this message finds you well. I am reaching out to inquire as to whether you might potentially be available for a brief conversation next week.”

**After**

“Hi Alex, do you have 20 minutes to talk next week?”

**Change:** Removed ceremonial wording without changing the ask. Do not use this level of informality if the audience or setting requires more formality.

## Keep necessary nuance

**Before**

“This plan will work.”

**Bad rewrite**

“This plan might potentially work, depending on a variety of factors.”

**Better rewrite**

“This plan should work if the vendor meets the stated latency target. We haven't verified that in production yet.”

**Change:** The goal is calibrated certainty, not more or less hedging by default.

## Don't over-humanize

**Before**

“The query timed out because the table scan touched too many rows.”

**Bad rewrite**

“Yeah, the query kind of fell over because it had to chew through a ton of rows.”

**Better rewrite**

“The query timed out because the full table scan processed too many rows. Add or verify an index that supports the filter, then compare the query plan.”

**Change:** Natural technical writing is clear and specific; it doesn't need forced slang.
